Before and after: UI SDK

Pick a place above

Each button sends both halves to the same board. Do the same thing with your hands on each side. Keys go to the side under the pointer (its label is underlined).

Keys that changed, what was not converted, and how these frames differ from real windows

Arrow keys on the board move focus between cards. To move a card: Option + arrow (add Shift for 10 steps). Previous / next Tinka on a board: [ and ]. Option + ← / → now moves the focused card. The path menu in an opened item opens on click, not on hover.

Left as they were: the top bar of a board, the opened-item view itself, the Inspector panels (their controls did change), the board map, the marquee, the opened item's zoom % menu, the card menu on legacy boards, and the hints on buttons inside cards. Pointer pan, zoom and drag were not changed on purpose.

Both sides are the real app, each on its own copy of your boards, laid out at a desktop width and scaled to fit (made for a full-screen window, 1440 px wide or more; Size changes the scale). What you change on one side does not happen on the other, and Reload both does not undo it. Settings such as Appearance are kept separately from your other windows. A few keys the browser keeps for itself (F6 among them) may not reach a side; try them in the 44221 window if so.
